Approach

Three promises we actually keep.

Most dental anxiety is really anxiety about surprise — surprise pain, surprise bills, surprise procedures. We designed the practice to remove the surprises.

  • You see what we see

    Intraoral camera and X-rays go straight to the screen in front of you. Nothing is diagnosed out of sight.

  • Written costs, before we start

    Every plan comes as an itemised estimate. If something changes mid-treatment, we stop and ask first.

  • Gentle is a technique, not a slogan

    Topical before every injection, slow-delivery anaesthetic, and a stop signal you can raise at any moment.

Your dentist

One dentist, start to finish.

You are seen by the same person at every visit — the one who took your first X-ray, who knows which side of your jaw is sensitive, and who will still be there when the crown needs checking in five years.

Dr. Kusum trained in conservative dentistry and endodontics, and has spent the last decade building a practice around a simple idea: that patients make better decisions when they are given the whole picture and enough time to think about it.

What a first visit looks like

Four steps. No surprises.

  1. Listen

    Fifteen minutes on what brought you in, what you've had done before, and what you're worried about.

  2. Look

    Full examination, camera, and X-rays only where they change the decision.

  3. Explain

    Findings on screen, options side by side, itemised costs, and the do-nothing option included honestly.

  4. Treat

    Only once you've agreed — and you can take the plan home to think about it first.

Questions

Things people ask before booking.

Will it hurt?

For most routine work, no. We use topical gel before any injection and deliver anaesthetic slowly, which is what makes the difference. You can raise a hand to stop at any point, and we will.

How much does a check-up cost?

A full examination including X-rays where needed is a fixed fee, quoted when you book. Any treatment that follows is itemised in writing before it starts.

Do you see emergencies?

Yes. We hold slots open every weekday for pain, swelling, a lost filling or a broken front tooth. Call the practice directly rather than using the form.

Do you treat nervous patients?

Often. Tell us when you book and we will schedule a longer, treatment-free first appointment — just conversation and an examination, nothing else.

What about children?

We see children from their first tooth onwards. Early visits are short, familiar and deliberately uneventful, which is the point.
